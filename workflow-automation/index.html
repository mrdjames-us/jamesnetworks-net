Automate the busywork · keep the judgment human

Inbox, quotes, and tickets — with gates

Most “AI automation” fails the same way: it speeds up a messy process and then freelances the consequential step. We do the opposite. Map the work. Kill busywork. Automate the repeatable parts. Put a person on money, access, deletes, and anything else that should not fire on its own.

Inbox

Triage, drafts, and follow-up nudges — with a human reading anything that commits the business. The agent can sort and summarize. It does not send the apology, the price, or the legal language unless you say so.

Quotes

Pull history, fill the boilerplate, flag missing pieces. A person still owns the number. If a quote can move money, it waits on an approval. That is not optional.

Tickets

Classify, suggest next steps, draft the customer update. Access changes and destructive actions stay behind a gate. Your techs keep the judgment. The queue gets lighter.

The gates

Money. Access. Deletes. Plus whatever else your shop treats as irreversible. We name those gates in the Assess step so they are not an afterthought in the Automate step. See services for how discovery and enablement wrap around this, or AI consulting in Clinton if you want the local picture.
