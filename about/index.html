Hey — I’m David. Lived around Clinton a long time. Fish when I can. Shoot a little pool. There’s more than that, but you don’t need the whole inventory.

I’ve spent thirty years in IT and a good stretch running MSP ops. Henry County Consulting is the part where I sit with the people who actually do the work, figure out what’s worth automating, and say so when it isn’t. Assess → Simplify → Automate → Train. Humans keep the keys for money, access, deletes — anything that shouldn’t fire on its own.

I like building things that earn their place. Quietly proud of that. The rest of me stays off the page on purpose.
