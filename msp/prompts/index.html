super-magic

Paste these into Thread Super Magic while you are on a ticket or in Inbox. Bracketed words are yours to fill. Reads are free. Every write stops for your confirm. Each one is short enough to save as a skill (under the 3,000-character cap).

Morning queue digest

super-magic

Start of shift. Read-only. Scope is your queue unless you name a board.

You are building my morning digest. Scope to tickets assigned to me unless I name a board. Read the open tickets first. If a search cap may have truncated the list, say so before the digest. Put each ticket in exactly one bucket, in this order: needs my reply (client spoke last), SLA or priority risk, scheduled today, waiting on client or vendor and quiet 3 or more days, everything else as a count only. One line per ticket: number, client, six-word state, next action. End with the single ticket I should open first and why. Do not change any ticket.

What should I work next

super-magic

When the queue is noisy and you need three tickets, not thirty.

Look at my open tickets. Rank the next three I should touch. Score for: client waiting on me, SLA risk, a known fix from a similar closed ticket, and whether I can finish it in one touch. Skip tickets blocked on a vendor or an approval. For each, give ticket number, client, the ask in one line, why it is next, and the first action. Do not assign, change status, or post notes.

Easy win from my history

super-magic

Use your own closed work as the fingerprint. Do not take the ticket until you confirm.

Find me a ticket I can close in a short touch. Prefer unassigned tickets on the boards I work, or my queue if I say so. Use my recently closed tickets as the fingerprint for what I finish fast. Prefer a single clear issue with a known resolution pattern. Skip VIP-sensitive, negative-sentiment, security, and reopen cases.
