People ask what I do. The honest answer is several things, and they are not the same job.

Henry County Consulting

The paid practice. I sit with whoever actually does the job, map the real process, and only then talk about automation. Assess → Simplify → Automate → Train. Humans stay in the loop for money, access, deletes, and anything else that shouldn't fire on its own. If AI isn't the answer, I say that on the first call.

That's henrycountyconsulting.com. Clinton and Henry County first. Zoom after we know the work.

AI for Missouri

Education. Workshops, coaching, the "how do we even start" conversations for people who don't need a consultant every week. aiformissouri.com. Not an engagement. A classroom.

DJ Prints 3D

Physical goods. Custom chips, coins, pocket markers. PETG, not stickers. djprints3d.com. This is the tinkering that ships in a box.

League tools

I captain APA teams and I got tired of the scorekeeping and lineup work living in my head. So I built tools: APA Captain, plus 8-ball and 9-ball scoring. Lab software. Useful to me, maybe useful to you. Not the consulting offer.

This site

The notebook. What I built this week, what I learned, what I'm chasing (APA Championship in Vegas, more time on the water, a practice I can explain to a neighbor). If you only read one URL of mine, make it this one.

I keep the doors labeled because I have watched too many shops glue five businesses onto one homepage and then wonder why nobody knows what to do next. Same rule I use on client work: don't automate a mess. Don't publish one either.
