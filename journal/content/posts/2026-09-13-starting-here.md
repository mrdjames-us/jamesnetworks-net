This used to be a company brochure. I'm done with that.

[jamesnetworks.net](https://www.jamesnetworks.net/) is my site. I live in Clinton, Missouri. I have thirty years in IT, a consulting practice, an education company, a 3D print shop, some league apps, and a pool cue I take too seriously. This is where I'll write about what I'm actually doing — the work, the learning, the water, the table, and the rest of it.

If you came here for paid consulting, that's [Henry County Consulting](https://www.henrycountyconsulting.com/). It has its own door. Workshops live at [AI for Missouri](https://www.aiformissouri.com/). I keep those separate on purpose. Mixing the notebook with the invoice is how you end up with a site that doesn't tell the truth.

## What I'll write

Weekly, as a rule. Every weekly post highlights **one hosted build you can open and see**. Not a screenshot of a slide. A URL. This week that's this site.

Some weeks that's a real post. Some weeks it's a short note. Daily when the work is interesting enough to share and I can do it without padding.

I won't write "10 AI tips." I will write when I shipped something, when I was wrong, when a local shop taught me a better way to see a process, when a match went the way it shouldn't have, or when I want a record of the season I'm in.

Faith will show up when it's relevant. I'm a Christian. I don't use that as a heading. It shapes how I show up: honest about limits, no hype, stay humble, do the work.

## How this is built

Markdown files. A small build script. Cloudflare Pages. Same boring stack I use everywhere else. If you want to follow along in a reader, there's an [RSS feed](/rss.xml).
