Services

What we work on together

Practical AI for small and mid-size businesses — especially shops that live in email, tickets, CRM, and spreadsheets. Same method every time. Humans approve money, access, and deletes. No hype, no SaaS pitch, no “agent army” slide.

Workflow discovery

Find the routines that eat the week; pick by pain and payoff — before writing a single prompt. Output is a clear plan, not a tool list.

Agents & automations

Named jobs with clear ownership in tools you already use. Inbox triage, follow-ups, quoting nudges, ticket assist, reporting digests. If a plain workflow will do, we don’t dress it up as an agent. Inbox, quotes, tickets.

Ops patterns for service businesses

Service-desk hygiene, lifecycle summaries, glue between tools a lean team already lives in. MSP-ops discipline applied to AI.

Team enablement

Hands-on sessions so your folks know when to use AI, when to escalate, and how to keep quality up. Your team owns the playbook.
