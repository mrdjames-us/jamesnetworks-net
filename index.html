The method

Assess → Simplify → Automate → Train

Same ops discipline you’d use on a messy service desk — applied to AI. We don’t start with a chatbot. We start with the work.

  1. 01

    Assess

    I sit with whoever does the job. Triggers, handoffs, exceptions — and what should stay human.

  2. 02

    Simplify

    Some steps shouldn’t exist. Automating a broken process just makes the mess go faster.

  3. 03

    Automate

    Agents and workflows in tools you already use. Approvals for money, access, deletes — AI doesn’t freestyle the consequential stuff. How the gates work.

  4. 04

    Train

    Your people run the playbook when I’m gone. No mystery vendor.
