Straight answers

Short version of the practice. If you want the local picture, see AI consulting in Clinton. If you want the menu, see services.

What is Henry County Consulting?

Paid, do-it-with-you workflow and agent consulting led by David James in Clinton, Missouri. We walk Assess → Simplify → Automate → Train. Humans approve money, access, and deletes.

Are you the same as AI for Missouri?

No. AI for Missouri is statewide workshops and coaching. Henry County Consulting is the paid consulting practice. Same person, different offer — workshops are labeled as workshops in the footer.

What about James Networks?

James Networks is the product lab — apps and experiments. Consulting does not live there. If you landed on a lab URL from this domain, it should send you to jamesnetworks.net.

Who is this for?

Owners and ops leads in Clinton, Henry County, and west-central Missouri, plus remote clients who want a real person across the table. Lean Main Street teams, not enterprise RFPs.

What do you actually automate?

Inbox, quotes, tickets, follow-ups, reporting digests — named jobs in tools you already use. We do not automate a broken process. We do not let a model freestyle the consequential step.

What stays human?

Money, access, deletes, and anything irreversible. You can add gates. The point of the brand line is literal: turn the aggravations into automations; keep the judgment human.

Do you sell a SaaS product here?

No. This site is consulting. No platform pitch, no seat licenses, no mystery vendor lock-in. Your team should be able to explain what runs when I leave.

How do we start?

Thirty minutes. Bring one painful routine. Coffee in Clinton or Zoom, by appointment. Book a call, email david@henrycountyconsulting.com, or call (660) 869-2868.

Do you publish workshop pricing here?

No. Workshop pricing belongs on aiformissouri.com. This site does not quote class rates.
