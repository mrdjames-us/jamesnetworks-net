AI consulting for Clinton and Henry County

I’m David James. I live and work in Clinton. Henry County Consulting is the paid, do-it-with-you practice — not a workshop series and not a product lab. If your week disappears into email, quotes, tickets, or chasing callbacks, we sit down (coffee in town or Zoom) and look at the actual job before anyone writes a prompt.

Statewide classes live at AI for Missouri — workshops. Apps and experiments live at James Networks — the lab. This site is the consulting front door.

Who this is for

Owners and ops leads in Clinton, Henry County, and nearby west-central Missouri shops that already run lean. Trade, professional services, Main Street — teams that cannot afford a mystery vendor and do not want another SaaS pitch. Remote is fine after we know the work. First conversations are local on purpose.

How a first engagement actually starts

Bring one painful routine. We walk Assess → Simplify → Automate → Train. I sit with whoever does the job. We map triggers, handoffs, and exceptions — and we name what must stay human. Money, access, and deletes do not fire on their own. If AI is the wrong tool, I will say so and we stop there.
