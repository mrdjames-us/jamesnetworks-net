How a first engagement actually starts

Bring one painful routine. We walk Assess → Simplify → Automate → Train. I sit with whoever does the job. We map triggers, handoffs, and exceptions — and we name what must stay human. Money, access, and deletes do not fire on their own. If AI is the wrong tool, I will say so and we stop there.
