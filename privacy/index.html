How this site handles information

This is a small consulting site for Henry County Consulting (David James, Clinton, MO 64735). We do not sell lists. We do not run an ad network on these pages.

What we collect

  • Email or phone you choose to send — if you write or call, that conversation is the record.
  • Booking details you enter on Calendly when you schedule a call. Calendly is a third party with its own privacy policy.
  • Basic, cookieless-style site analytics via Umami hosted at stats.aiformissouri.com, used to see which pages are read — not to profile you across the web.
